About

A gentle, structured look at your habits.

The assessment asks about your daily routine, social media use and stress. A trained statistical model compares your answers with patterns found in survey data and returns a single score.

Information

Please read before you begin.

Results describe what a model predicts from the answers you give. They cannot account for your personal history or circumstances. If you are worried about your wellbeing, please speak with a qualified health professional or someone you trust.
